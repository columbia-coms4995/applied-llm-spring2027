Project & exam details+

Three projects, one support case

  • MP 1 — Model adaptation. Compare a prompt baseline with SFT/LoRA on held-out examples.
  • MP 2 — Retrieval. Build a RAG pipeline, diagnose failures, and measure an improvement.
  • MP 3 — Agents. Compare an improved agent with the original and a fixed workflow; check success, cost, and judge reliability.

Well-supported negative results can earn full credit.

Exams

Midterm: meetings 1–6, in class. Short calculations, result interpretation, and engineering scenarios.

Final: cumulative, in person. Emphasis on evaluation, failure diagnosis, and justified decisions.

Project requirements and grading criteria will accompany each assignment.

Course information

Python, basic probability, and ML fundamentals. No prior deep learning or NLP course required.

Prerequisites, computing & AI tools+

You should have trained and evaluated at least one ML model. The course welcomes graduate students and advanced undergraduates with this background.

Assignments may need model APIs or GPUs; requirements and access options will be provided with each assignment.

AI coding assistants are permitted with disclosure. You remain responsible for correctness.

Reading & course details+

AI Engineering — Chip Huyen (2025). Recommended; purchase is not required.

Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch) — Sebastian Raschka (2024). Optional training background.
